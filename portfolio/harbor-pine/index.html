Frequently asked questions

Do you take my insurance?+

We work with most major PPO plans and file claims for you. Send us your plan before your visit, and we’ll explain your coverage in plain language before any treatment.

I haven’t been to a dentist in years. Is that okay?+

Yes. We begin with a comprehensive exam, review the findings with you, and build a treatment plan at a pace that works for you.

What if I have a dental emergency?+

Call us first thing. We keep same-day emergency slots open every weekday for pain, swelling, and broken or knocked-out teeth.

Can I bring my kids to the same office?+

Yes. Families can book back-to-back appointments, so everyone is in and out in one trip.

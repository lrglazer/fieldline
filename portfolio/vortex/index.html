About this project

This is a design example. VORTEX isn't real.

VORTEX is a made-up product. There's no ball to buy, no company and no drop list. I designed and built this site to show what I can do: product storytelling, interactive 3D, motion design and real engineering behind the visuals.

The flight lab and wind tunnel use simplified versions of real aerodynamics: drag, Magnus lift from backspin, and flow around a sphere. The specs are realistic for a golf ball, but none of them belong to an actual product.

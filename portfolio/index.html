Automation, in plain English.

Automation means software does the repetitive parts of your business for you: the copying, posting, updating and reminding you'd otherwise do by hand. I set it up once, connected to the tools you already use, and it runs every day without you.

Without automation

  • ✕Post every new product to Instagram and Facebook by hand
  • ✕Update stock on your website after every eBay or in-person sale
  • ✕Email receipts and order confirmations one by one
  • ✕Rebuild a sales spreadsheet every week
  • ✕Miss messages and offers when you're away from the computer
Automated

With automation

  • ✓New products post themselves, with photo, price and a buy link
  • ✓Stock syncs everywhere the moment something sells
  • ✓Payments, receipts and order records happen on their own
  • ✓A report lands in your inbox every morning
  • ✓Your phone buzzes for every sale, offer and new lead

CASE STUDY · H&S SPORTS CARDS

How I automated the whole business

H&S was selling cards on eBay, Instagram and at shows, tracking everything by hand. I built one system that runs it end to end: inventory, website, checkout, eBay, social posting and owner alerts. Then I made the owners a video guide so they could run it themselves.

Case study intro + the 2-minute owner guide I made for Steven & Harley.

One inventory, every channelEvery card lives in one database that the website, eBay and social all read from. Sold anywhere = sold everywhere, so nothing sells twice.
Checkout on autopilotStripe Buy Now links are created for every card, update when prices change, and mark the card sold with an order record, buyer receipt and owner alert.
One-click eBay listingA “Post on eBay” automation lists a card on their eBay account with Best Offer on; eBay sales sync back automatically.
Social that posts itselfNew cards auto-post to Instagram and the Facebook Page. AI reads each card photo, fills in the details and suggests a price from eBay sales.
Weekly GOAT ReportEvery Sunday, every card is re-priced against real sold comps, with a buy list and under/overpriced alerts.
Owners in the loopA daily business email, phone push notifications for sales, offers, messages and leads, and a home-screen app.

About this project

This is a design example. CADENCE isn't a real watch or brand.

I designed and built this concept site to show product storytelling, motion design and interactive graphics, with real mechanical engineering underneath. There's no watch for sale and no company behind it.

The movement is drawn and animated in code. Its gear ratios are real: the center wheel turns once an hour, the fourth wheel once a minute, and the escapement beats 8 times a second (28,800 per hour). The physical specs are realistic but made up.

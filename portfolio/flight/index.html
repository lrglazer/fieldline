A single sheet of paper takes off at sunrise and flies until midnight. Scroll to fold it, throw it and follow it across the sky.

Morning

Sunrise.

Seven folds turn a flat sheet into a wing. The crease along the middle becomes the keel, which keeps the plane flying straight.

Midday

Lift.

Air moving under the tilted wings is pushed down, so the plane is pushed up. Paper airplanes glide at about 1 meter down for every 8 meters forward. Enough speed and they can even loop.

city.

The light turns gold, then pink. One by one, windows light up below as the city gets ready for the night.

About this project

This is a design example.

Paper Flight is an animation experiment I designed and built to show motion design and scroll storytelling. It's not a product, a company or a real flight.

Every frame is generated in the browser with canvas: time of day, parallax layers, the folding animation, the flight path and the planes you throw.
